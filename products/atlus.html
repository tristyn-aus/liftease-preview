Atlus technical specifications

The Atlus combines premium design with advanced electric hybrid screw drive technology, offering a smooth, quiet ride with minimal pit requirements. It is fully contained, requires no oil, and features a state-of-the-art touch screen with a battery backup system.

Unrivalled engineering & design

The Atlus elevator incorporates cutting-edge hybrid screw drive technology for minimal footprint, silent operation, and supreme reliability.

  • 400 kg (5 person) capacity, wheelchair friendly

  • Low to No Pit Option (50mm – 150mm)

  • Electric Hybrid Screw Drive

  • Speed: 0.15 – 0.30 m/s

  • Fully Contained Screw System

  • Thicker pitch and lead for smoother ride

  • Quiet fully electric with backup battery drive

  • Touch Screen as Standard

  • Full glass tower options available
