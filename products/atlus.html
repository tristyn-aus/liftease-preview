FAQs

Atlus questions

Does the Atlus need a pit?

Only a shallow one. The Atlus has a low to no pit option, needing a pit depth of 50 mm to 150 mm.

How is the Atlus different from the Vector Pro?

The Atlus shares the Vector Pro load (400 kg, 5 person), door, cabin and sizing options, including cabins up to 1100 × 1350 mm. It uses an electric hybrid screw drive instead of hydraulics, travels at 0.15 to 0.30 m/s and needs only a 50 to 150 mm pit.

Does the Atlus use oil?

No. Its fully contained screw system means no oil mess and reduced maintenance requirements.

What power does the Atlus need?

Single phase 230VAC, like all our home elevators. Only the E1 commercial lift needs three phase power.

What happens if the power goes out?

The Atlus is quiet and fully electric, with a reliable backup battery drive.

How long does installation take?

Installation of the Atlus typically takes 1–2 weeks.
