What's included

What's included in professional elevator servicing

Professional elevator maintenance is comprehensive, covering every critical system and component. Here's what you can expect when Lift Ease services your elevator:

  • Safety System Inspections

    Comprehensive testing of emergency brakes, alarm systems, and backup power to ensure passenger safety.

  • Mechanical Component Checks

    Detailed examination of pulleys, cables, motors, and drive systems for wear and proper function.

  • Electrical System Testing

    Full diagnostic checks of control panels, wiring, sensors, and emergency communication systems.

  • Lubrication and Adjustments

    Precision lubrication and calibration of moving parts to reduce friction and extend component life.

  • Door and Gate Maintenance

    Alignment, adjustment, and testing of door mechanisms to prevent entrapment and ensure smooth operation.

  • Hydraulic System Checks

    Inspection of hydraulic fluid levels, pressure systems, and seals to prevent leaks and failures.

  • Emergency System Testing

    Verification that all emergency features, including battery backup and alarm systems, function correctly.

FAQs

Servicing questions

What maintenance is required?

We recommend a minimum of one service per year, twice-yearly for heavily used lifts, or as outlined by the manufacturer's servicing schedule, to keep your elevator in peak condition. This includes lubricating moving parts, checking safety systems, and ensuring all components are functioning correctly. We offer comprehensive service packages for your peace of mind.

What happens if the power goes out?

Home lifts have a battery backup that brings the car to the nearest landing and opens the door, so nobody is left sitting in a stopped lift.
