What to Know Before Installing a Home Elevator in Australia

22 August 2025

More and more Australian homeowners are discovering the transformative benefits of residential elevators. Beyond luxury, these lifts offer unparalleled accessibility, comfort, and can significantly enhance property value. But before you take the plunge, it’s essential to understand the key considerations for a smooth and successful installation.

Step 1: Understanding Regulations & Standards

In Australia, residential lifts must comply with specific safety and performance standards. The primary standard is AS1735.18 (Lifts, escalators and moving walks - Passenger and goods lifts - Lifts for persons with impaired mobility - Restricted use - Automatically controlled). This standard covers everything from design and construction to installation and maintenance. Navigating these regulations can be complex, but at Lift Ease, we handle all compliance aspects for our clients, ensuring your lift meets every Australian requirement.

Step 2: Choosing the Right Size & Model

Small vs. Large Cabins

Consider your needs and available space. Smaller cabins are ideal for single users or limited spaces, while larger options can accommodate wheelchairs, multiple passengers, or even furniture.

Open vs. Enclosed Cabins

Open-style lifts offer a minimalist aesthetic and can feel less intrusive, while enclosed cabins provide a more traditional elevator experience and often greater privacy.

Space-Saving Solutions

Even if space is limited, Lift Ease offers innovative solutions designed to fit seamlessly into existing homes, including compact designs that require minimal structural modifications.

Step 3: Safety & Warranty

Built-in Safety Features

Modern home lifts come equipped with advanced safety features, including emergency stop buttons, battery backup for power outages, door interlocks, and overload sensors. At Lift Ease, your safety is our top priority.

Importance of Regular Servicing

To ensure optimal performance and safety, regular servicing is crucial. Our lifts come with a warranty that requires “Authorised Vendor Servicing Only.” This ensures that only qualified technicians maintain your lift, preserving its longevity and your peace of mind.

Step 4: Installation Process

The installation of a home elevator is a multi-step process, typically involving:

  • **Site Inspection:** Our experts assess your home’s layout and structural requirements.
  • **Design & Customisation:** We work with you to design a lift that perfectly matches your home’s aesthetic and your needs.
  • **Installation:** Our certified technicians handle the entire installation process with minimal disruption.
  • **Testing & Handover:** Rigorous testing ensures everything is perfect before we hand over your new lift.

At Lift Ease, we manage the entire process from start to finish, providing a stress-free experience for our customers.

Step 5: Cost & Maintenance

General Overview of Costs

The cost of a home elevator varies widely depending on the model, size, features, and installation complexity. While it’s a significant investment, remember the long-term benefits in accessibility, convenience, and property value. Contact us for a personalized quote.

Ongoing Maintenance Requirements

Like any sophisticated machinery, home lifts require regular maintenance. We recommend a minimum of one service per year, twice-yearly for heavily used lifts, or as outlined by the manufacturer’s servicing schedule, to ensure it remains in peak condition and to comply with warranty requirements.
