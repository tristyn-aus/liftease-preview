Home Elevator Installation Timeline in Melbourne: What to Expect Week by Week

6 July 2026

One of the most common questions Melbourne homeowners ask when considering a home elevator is: how long will the whole thing take? The honest answer is that there are two very different timelines to understand — the on-site installation itself, and the full project journey from first consultation to the day you step into your new lift. Both matter, and confusing the two is the source of most homeowner frustration.

This guide breaks down the home elevator installation timeline in Melbourne stage by stage, including how long on-site installation actually takes for different lift models, what drives the variation, and what you can do to keep things moving.

On-Site Installation vs the Full Project Timeline

The on-site installation — the physical work of assembling and commissioning your lift — is just one part of a longer process. Depending on the lift you choose, it may take as little as one to two days or as long as one to two weeks. But that work typically happens in weeks eight to twelve of a project that starts the moment you first make contact with a supplier.

The full home elevator installation timeline in Melbourne, from initial consultation through to handover and certification, generally runs between ten and sixteen weeks. The single biggest variable is not the installation itself — it is the permit and approval phase, which sits entirely outside your installer’s control once the application is lodged.

What Determines How Long Your Installation Takes?

Three factors drive the on-site installation timeline for any home elevator in Melbourne.

The Size of the Lift

Compact, single-entry lifts require far fewer components and less structural integration than large platform lifts. The Lift Ease Levare — our purpose-built compact home elevator — can typically be installed in about 2 days. It is designed from the ground up for efficient installation in modern Australian homes, with a streamlined component set that experienced installers can commission quickly without sacrificing quality or safety.

Larger platform lifts, such as the Lift Ease Vector Pro and Zenith Ultra, are a different proposition. These lifts are engineered for greater capacity, more demanding use, and higher-specification finishes — and most installations are completed within 2 weeks, some models within 1 week.

Total Travel: Floors and Shaft Height

Every additional floor a lift serves adds complexity. More travel means more guide rail to fit and align, more cable or hydraulic line to run, more landing door assemblies to install, and more commissioning work to ensure precise levelling at every floor. A two-storey installation is substantially simpler than a three- or four-storey one, and this is reflected directly in on-site time. For larger lifts serving multiple floors, this factor alone can push the on-site phase toward the longer end of the one-to-two-week range.

Level of Fit-Out and Finish

A standard cab finish — clean panels, brushed steel fixtures, functional lighting — installs faster than a fully bespoke interior. Custom mirror walls, premium door surrounds, integrated lighting systems, and tailored cab dimensions all add time at the finishing stage. If your Zenith Ultra is being fitted out to match a high-specification renovation, plan for the upper end of the installation window. If you are choosing a practical, well-finished standard configuration, your installer can work more quickly.

The Melbourne Installation Timeline: Week by Week

Weeks 1–2: Consultation and Site Assessment

The project begins with a detailed site visit. Your Lift Ease consultant will assess your home’s structure, measure available space, discuss lift type options, and walk you through what the installation will involve for your specific property. For Melbourne homes — particularly older Victorian and Edwardian properties in suburbs like Fitzroy, Northcote, or Kew — the structural assessment is especially important, as older building stock can present unique considerations around load-bearing walls and floor construction.

Weeks 2–4: Design, Specification, and Product Selection

Once the site assessment is complete, your Lift Ease team develops a full specification: shaft dimensions (or shaft-free configuration for applicable models), structural requirements, electrical supply needs, cab finish selections, and any custom features. This is also when you lock in your product choice — whether that is the compact and efficient Levare, the capable Vector Pro, or the premium Zenith Ultra — which directly sets your on-site installation timeline.

Weeks 3–10: Permits and Building Approvals

In Victoria, any home elevator installation involving structural work — including shaft construction — requires a building permit issued by a registered Building Surveyor. This is the phase that varies most in Melbourne. In straightforward cases, approvals can be turned around in two to three weeks. In heritage overlay areas — parts of South Yarra, Carlton, North Melbourne, and Fitzroy among others — additional heritage considerations can extend this to six weeks or more.

Lift Ease prepares and submits all permit documentation on your behalf and liaises with your Building Surveyor throughout. You will be kept informed, but you do not need to manage this process yourself.

Weeks 6–10: Site Preparation

While permits are being processed — or immediately after approval, depending on timing — any necessary site preparation begins. For shaft-based lifts, this means structural carpentry to build or adapt the shaft, plus a dedicated electrical circuit installed by a licensed electrician. Lift Ease coordinates trusted Melbourne trades for both, so you are not managing multiple contractors independently. For shaft-free models like the Levare in certain configurations, this phase is minimal and adds little to the overall timeline.

Weeks 8–14: On-Site Installation

This is when your lift arrives and goes in. For the Levare, expect about 2 days of on-site work — your home is minimally disrupted and the lift is operational quickly. For larger lifts such as the Vector Pro or Zenith Ultra, most installations are completed within 2 weeks, with the exact duration depending on the number of floors served and the level of finish specified.

Lift Ease installers work methodically through mechanical fit, electrical connection, door alignment, cab installation, and initial testing. Access to the installation area should be clear for the duration, and the immediate surrounds will need to remain accessible to the team throughout each day of work.

Weeks 10–16: Final Inspection, Certification, and Handover

Once installation is complete, the lift must be inspected and certified under AS 1735 — the Australian Standard governing residential lift installations — before it can be commissioned for use. In Victoria, this inspection is carried out by a registered lift inspector. Lift Ease arranges this appointment and manages all certification paperwork. Once signed off, we hand over your operating manuals, warranty documentation, and a plain-language briefing on your ongoing maintenance obligations under Victorian regulations.

What You Can Do to Keep Your Melbourne Installation on Track

The biggest cause of timeline blowout in Melbourne home elevator projects is not anything the installer does — it is delays in the permit phase caused by incomplete documentation or slow response to surveyor queries. Responding promptly to any information requests from your Building Surveyor is the single most effective thing you can do to keep the project moving.

Beyond that, having your electrical supply sorted early, ensuring the installation area is clear and accessible on the agreed start date, and making finish selections decisively during the design phase will all help your project track toward the shorter end of the timeline range rather than the longer one.
