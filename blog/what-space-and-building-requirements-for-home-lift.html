What Space and Building Requirements Are Needed for Installing a Home Lift?

25 November 2025

Dreaming of adding a home lift to your property? It’s an excellent investment in convenience, accessibility, and property value. However, before you start picking finishes, it’s crucial to understand the space and building requirements. This guide will walk you through the essential considerations to ensure a smooth installation process.

Key Dimensions and Space Considerations

The physical dimensions are often the first hurdle. While exact figures vary by model, here’s a general overview:

  • Shaft Size (Footprint):

    This refers to the external dimensions of the space required for the lift structure. For a standard 2-person lift, expect a footprint around 1100mm x 1200mm. Larger lifts for wheelchairs or multiple passengers will naturally require more space.

  • Cabin Size Options:

    The internal space where you stand or sit. Common cabin sizes range from 800mm x 1000mm to 1100mm x 1400mm. Consider your needs: will it carry only people, or also groceries, luggage, or a wheelchair?

  • Pit Depth:

    Most home lifts require a shallow pit (a small depression in the floor) at the lowest level to allow the lift car to sit flush with the floor. This typically ranges from 100mm to 200mm. Some models offer pit-less or very shallow pit options, ideal for existing homes.

  • Overhead Clearance:

    This is the minimum clear height required above the top floor where the lift will stop. It ensures enough space for the lift mechanism and the car’s safe movement. Typically, you’ll need between 2400mm to 3000mm, depending on the lift type and model.

Electrical and Structural Considerations

Beyond physical space, your home’s infrastructure needs to be ready:

  • Electrical Requirements:

    Most residential lifts operate on standard single-phase 240V power, similar to other major appliances. However, some larger or faster models might require a dedicated circuit. A qualified electrician will need to install a power point and potentially a dedicated circuit breaker near the lift motor (often at the top of the shaft or in a nearby cupboard).

  • Structural Considerations:

    Your home’s structure must be able to support the weight of the lift and its contents, as well as handle the operational forces. This typically involves reinforcing floor openings and ensuring adequate support for the lift shaft (whether self-supporting or integrated into the building structure). An engineer’s assessment is often required, especially for existing homes.

Comparing Lift Types: What Are Their Requirements?

Different lift technologies have different space demands:

  • Hydraulic Lifts:

    Often require a machine room or a space for the hydraulic power unit (can be a small cabinet). They typically need a deeper pit and more overhead clearance than traction lifts.

  • Traction (or MRL - Machine Room Less) Lifts:

    More compact, as the motor is integrated into the shaft. They generally require less pit depth and overhead clearance, making them popular for residential retrofits.

  • Shaftless Lifts (e.g., Pneumatic Vacuum Lifts):

    These are the most compact, often requiring no pit or machine room. They can fit into tighter spaces, making them ideal for homes where traditional lifts are not feasible.

Lift Ease Specific Requirements

At Lift Ease, our range of premium residential elevators is designed for both new constructions and existing homes. While each model has specific requirements, we focus on providing flexible solutions. Our self-supporting structures mean less reliance on your home’s existing walls, and many models offer minimal pit and overhead requirements. The Importance of Early Planning

Planning is paramount!

Integrating a home lift is most straightforward when planned during the architectural design phase of a new build or major renovation. This allows for optimal placement and seamless integration of structural and electrical needs. However, retrofitting is very common and entirely feasible with proper planning and an experienced team.
