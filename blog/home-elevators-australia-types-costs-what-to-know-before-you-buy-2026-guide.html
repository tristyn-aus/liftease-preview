Home Elevators Australia: Types, Costs & What to Know Before You Buy (2026 Guide)

16 June 2026

More Australian homeowners are installing residential lifts than ever — whether it’s to future-proof a multi-storey home, support an ageing family member, or simply add a touch of luxury. But with so many options out there, where do you start?

Here’s what you need to know.

Types of Home Elevators

There are four main types available in Australia:

  • Hydraulic — smooth and reliable; many need a machine room, though our Vector Pro needs only a small cupboard for the pump
  • All-electric (traction) — quieter, energy-efficient, no machine room needed
  • Vacuum (pneumatic) — stylish and self-supporting, minimal structural work required
  • Compact / shaftless — the easiest to retrofit, with little to no structural modifications

How Much Does a Home Elevator Cost?

Prices vary depending on type, number of floors, cabin size, and customisation. In Australia, you can generally expect to pay anywhere from $36,000 for a basic compact model up to $100,000+ for a fully custom premium lift. Annual servicing typically runs $1500–$2,500 depending on the model and size.

Does a Home Elevator Add Value?

Almost always, yes. A residential lift is a genuine point of difference in the property market — particularly for multi-storey homes — and is increasingly sought after by buyers prioritising accessibility and comfort.

Our Recommendation: The Vector Pro

If you want a premium home elevator without the premium price tag, the Vector Pro is hard to beat. It draws around 14 A depending on configuration, compared with the standard 32 A typical of electric traction, our showroom unit measured an impressively quiet 42 dB, and it features a fully leak-proof design so there’s no risk of hydraulic fluid damage in your home.

It’s built to last, typically installed in 1–2 weeks, and comes with full custom interior options so it complements your home’s style perfectly.

Looking for Something More Compact? Meet the Levare.

For homeowners after a more space-conscious or budget-friendly solution, the Levare is an outstanding choice. Starting from just $36,000, it features a fully energy-efficient motor with a belt-driven system for a smooth, quiet ride.

Best of all — no pit required, minimal to no builder work, and installation is typically completed in about 2 days. It’s the simplest way to add a lift to an existing home.
