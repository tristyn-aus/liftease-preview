Features

Vector Pro key features

Every component of the Vector Pro is crafted with precision, safety, and user experience in mind. Discover the innovations that set it apart.

  • Leak-Proof Ram Design

    Our innovative ram design guarantees zero leaks, spills, or odours. Ever. Enjoy peace of mind with a clean, reliable, and maintenance-free hydraulic system.

  • Silent Submerged Motor

    The heart of the Vector Pro is its submerged motor, engineered for whisper-quiet operation. It eliminates noise and vibration, ensuring a tranquil home environment.

  • Smart Cabin Touch Panels

    Modern, intuitive, and stylish. Our smart touch panels provide a seamless user experience, with crystal-clear displays and effortless control at your fingertips.

  • Smooth Rides with Advanced Rollers

    Experience an exceptionally smooth and stable ride. Our long-lasting, advanced roller system minimises friction and absorbs bumps for ultimate comfort.

  • Safety Braking System

    Your safety is paramount. The Vector Pro is equipped with a robust, reliable braking system that provides instant, secure stopping power when you need it most.
