Features

Zenith Ultra key features

Every component of the Zenith Ultra is crafted with precision, safety, and user experience in mind. Discover the innovations that set it apart.

  • High Speed Energy Efficient Motor Drive

    Our advanced motor drive delivers unparalleled speed and efficiency while consuming minimal power, providing smooth, swift, and eco-friendly vertical transport.

  • Advanced Drive System

    Our state-of-the-art drive system ensures precision control, delivering a consistently smooth and responsive ride experience.

  • Smart Cabin Touch Panels

    Modern, intuitive, and stylish. Our smart touch panels provide a seamless user experience, with crystal-clear displays and effortless control at your fingertips.

  • Smooth Rides with Advanced Rollers

    Experience an exceptionally smooth and stable ride. Our long-lasting, advanced roller system minimises friction and absorbs bumps for ultimate comfort.

  • Safety Braking System

    Your safety is paramount. The Zenith Ultra is equipped with a robust, reliable braking system that provides instant, secure stopping power when you need it most.
