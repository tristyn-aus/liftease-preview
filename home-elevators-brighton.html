FAQs

Home lifts in Brighton: questions

Do you install home lifts in Brighton?

Yes. We supply, install and service home elevators in Brighton and across Melbourne. Our Keilor East showroom is about 33 km by road, around 37 minutes in light traffic.

Do I need a permit for a home lift in Brighton?

In Victoria, installing a lift in a home is building work, so you'll generally need a building permit issued by a registered building surveyor before anything starts. Heritage overlays apply in parts of the area: an internal lift usually doesn't touch the streetscape at all, which is why it's often an easier approval than a rear addition, but it's still a conversation to have with your council (Bayside City Council) before you commit.

Can you put a lift in an existing house?

Yes, a large percentage of home lifts are retrofitted. Most modern home lifts need only a shallow pit, and the Atlus has a low to no pit option of 50 to 150 mm.

How long does installation take?

Home lifts such as the Vector Pro, Zenith Ultra and Atlus typically take 1–2 weeks to install, Levare lifts about 2 days, and commercial lifts 2–4 weeks.

Can I see a lift before I buy?

Yes. Visiting our showroom at 61 Slater Parade, Keilor East is the best way to understand the quality behind every Lift Ease elevator. Open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.
