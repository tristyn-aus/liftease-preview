Costs

How much does a home lift cost in Victoria?

A home lift in Victoria typically costs $36,000 to $100,000+. Where your project lands depends on the lift type, levels served, shaft, cabin size and finishes. Final pricing depends on your property.

What affects the price

  • Lift type and drive: Hydraulic (Vector Pro), hybrid screw drive (Atlus) and belt-drive (Zenith Ultra) lifts suit different spaces and budgets.
  • Number of levels served: The Vector Pro serves up to 4 stops over 10 m; the Zenith Ultra up to 6 levels (G+5) over 15 m.
  • Shaft requirements: Whether you supply the shaft (shaft by client) or we supply a structural tower (shaft by Lift Ease), plus the pit: 120 mm for the Vector Pro, 50 to 150 mm for the Atlus, 150 to 300 mm for the Zenith Ultra.
  • Cabin dimensions: Standard cabins range from 750 × 700 mm up to 1100 × 1400 mm, with bespoke sizing available.
  • Interior finish: Standard finishes, or Elegance upgrades such as touchscreen controls and premium cabin designs.

Cost questions

How much does a home lift cost in Victoria?

A home lift typically costs $36,000 to $100,000+ in Victoria, depending on the lift type, number of levels, shaft requirements, cabin size and interior finish. We do a site assessment before quoting.

Can funding help pay for a home lift?

Yes, for eligible people. NDIS home modification funding can cover a lift where it is assessed as reasonable and necessary, and Support at Home AT-HM can contribute up to $15,390. An occupational therapist has to prescribe the work.

What ongoing costs should I plan for?
