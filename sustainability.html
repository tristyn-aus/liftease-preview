Sustainability

Our commercial sustainability focus

Operational efficiency and energy savings for modern commercial facilities.

Request Energy Specs Get a Quote

Commercial lifts consume significant energy in high-use environments like office buildings and shopping centres. Our goal is to minimise consumption over the lift's lifetime through smart design, efficient components, and responsible material choices.

For facility managers and property owners, sustainability isn't just about environmental compliance, it's about reducing operational costs, maximising ROI, and engineering that works.

  • Less energy, every day
  • Cleaner by design
  • Built to last, lighter on the building

Energy

Less energy, every day

  • Standby Mode

    Even in busy shopping centres and office buildings, commercial lifts spend significant idle time between peak hours. Our lifts drop to a low power state during these periods, and this power reduction directly impacts and significantly lowers your ongoing operational costs.

  • Auto-off Lighting System

    Car lighting and ventilation fans automatically shut down after brief idle periods. Across a fleet of commercial lifts, these cumulative energy savings and reduced wear translate to significantly lower maintenance costs for your facility management teams.

  • LED Lighting

    Equipped with high-efficiency LED lighting, our cabins draw significantly less power than halogen alternatives. In 24/7 commercial settings where multiple lifts operate continuously, this provides clear ROI and substantial cost savings over years of operation for facility managers.

  • Limiting Unnecessary Travel

    In high-traffic commercial environments, our smart call logic prevents empty runs and optimises travel paths. This advanced routing reduces energy waste and ensures maximum efficiency in busy office and retail settings.

  • Low Power Draw

    Designed for maximum efficiency, many of our solutions mean commercial buildings do not need expensive three-phase electrical upgrades. This dramatically reduces installation costs and complexity for your commercial projects.

  • Solar and Battery Compatibility

    Commercial buildings can easily leverage rooftop solar installations to power our efficient lifts. Coupled with battery backup systems for emergency power in multi-storey commercial buildings, this provides a forward-looking sustainability strategy for enterprise clients.

Design

Cleaner by design

  • Green Materials and Components

    We utilise recyclable materials, low-VOC finishes, and responsibly sourced parts for large commercial installations. This approach aligns perfectly with the environmental responsibility and ESG commitments required by enterprise clients.

  • Gearless Permanent Magnet Drive / VVVF Control

    Advanced Variable Voltage Variable Frequency controls deliver smoother, quieter rides while maximising efficiency gains in commercial high-cycle-count environments where lifts operate continuously throughout the day.

  • No Hydraulic Oil

    Our traction and screw-drive systems eliminate the need for hydraulic oil. For commercial operators and property management teams, this means easier environmental compliance, avoiding messy disposal obligations, and a significantly reduced maintenance burden.

Lifetime

Built to last, lighter on the building

  • Longevity and Serviceability

    Designed to last 20+ years, our lifts ensure long-term operational reliability while reducing replacement costs and total cost of ownership (TCO). We provide comprehensive servicing and parts support across VIC, NSW, SA, and TAS for commercial operators.

  • Reduced Building Impact

    Machine-room-less (MRL) and shallow-pit designs mean less excavation and structural work. For facility managers, this builds a strong business case by enabling faster installation times and substantially lower construction costs for commercial projects.
