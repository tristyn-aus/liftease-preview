Commercial DDA lifts

Which lift does your building need?

  • Zenith DDA: high-traffic commercial buildings. 1100 × 1400 mm car, up to 10 levels (G+9), machine-room-less electric traction, single phase 230VAC.
  • E1: low and mid-rise developments. Up to 2,500 kg, 1.0–2.0 m/s, up to 35 m travel, passenger and goods, three phase.
  • RB150: platform access over 600–3,000 mm with minimal construction, DDA compliant.
  • Commercial and DDA lifts are built to AS 1735 and Disability Discrimination Act access codes: compliant cabin dimensions, automatic sliding doors, tactile and Braille buttons, voice announcements and emergency lowering.

FAQs

Quick answers

What pit depth does a home lift need?

Atlus needs 50–150 mm (a low to no pit option), Vector Pro 120 mm and Zenith Ultra 150–300 mm. We confirm the exact figure for your site.

How long does a home lift installation take?

Vector Pro, Zenith Ultra and Atlus install in 1–2 weeks once the site is ready. Zenith DDA and E1 commercial lifts take 2–4 weeks.

Who builds the lift shaft?

Either: choose an Integrated Shaft built by your team to our shaft specifications, or a Structural Tower supplied by Lift Ease.

Do you service the lift after handover?

Yes. Our technicians service and repair all major brands with built in 24/7 SIM monitoring, and Vector Pro, Zenith Ultra, Atlus and Zenith DDA carry a 5-year warranty.

Are your lifts built to Australian Standards?

Yes. Our elevators are built to AS 1735, and our commercial lifts are DDA compliant.
