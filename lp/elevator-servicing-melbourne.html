FAQs

Quick answers

What maintenance is required?

We recommend a minimum of one service per year, twice-yearly for heavily used lifts, or as outlined by the manufacturer's servicing schedule.

What happens if the power goes out?

Home lifts have a battery backup that brings the car to the nearest landing and opens the door, so nobody is left sitting in a stopped lift.

Do you service pneumatic (vacuum) lifts?

Yes. We don't sell or install pneumatic lifts, but our technicians service and repair them, with extensive technical experience in vacuum lift systems.
