Commercial DDA lifts

Which lift does your building need?

  • Zenith DDA: high-traffic commercial buildings. 1100 × 1400 mm car, up to 10 levels (G+9), machine-room-less electric traction, single phase 230VAC.
  • E1: low and mid-rise developments. Up to 2,500 kg, 1.0–2.0 m/s, up to 35 m travel, passenger and goods, three phase.
  • RB150: platform access over 600–3,000 mm with minimal construction, DDA compliant.
  • Commercial and DDA lifts are built to AS 1735 and Disability Discrimination Act access codes: compliant cabin dimensions, automatic sliding doors, tactile and Braille buttons, voice announcements and emergency lowering.

FAQs

Quick answers

Can the cabin be sized to my design?

Yes. Vector Pro and Zenith Ultra offer complete bespoke sizing. Atlus comes in standard cabin sizes from 750 × 700 mm to 1100 × 1350 mm.

What are the shaft options?

An Integrated Shaft is built by the client’s builder to our shaft specifications. A Structural Tower is supplied by Lift Ease, with full glass tower options on Zenith Ultra and Atlus.

Which door types are available?

Zenith Ultra offers all 6 door types: automatic frameless, central and lateral sliding, automatic swing, manual swing and automatic bi swing. Vector Pro and Atlus use swing doors.

Where can I find lift specifications?

Each model has a published spec page: Vector Pro, Zenith Ultra, Atlus and Zenith DDA. Ask us for shaft drawings for your project.

Do you supply commercial DDA lifts?

Yes. Zenith DDA, E1 and RB150 are built to AS 1735 and Disability Discrimination Act access codes.
