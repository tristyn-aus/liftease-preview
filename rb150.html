FAQs

RB150 questions

How high can the RB150 lift?

From 600 mm to 3,000 mm.

What drive does the RB150 use?

An electric screw drive as standard, with a hydraulic variant available. Hydraulic suits a low-duty access lift like the RB150; our commercial passenger elevators use electric traction because of their much higher duty cycle.

Is the RB150 DDA compliant?

Yes. It is DDA compliant, ensuring safe usage for everyone.

Does the RB150 need building work?

Very little. It requires minimal to no construction, keeping installation quick and low-cost.

Where can the RB150 be used?

In homes, to bypass small staircases, and in schools, churches, clinics and small public buildings.

What power does the RB150 need?

Single phase 230VAC. Only the E1 commercial lift needs three phase power.

How long does installation take?

The RB150 is installed within a week, once the site is ready. Minimal construction keeps it quick.
