DDA & accessibility

DDA & accessibility solutions

Ensuring your building provides equal and dignified access for all individuals is a critical commercial requirement. We specialise in lifts designed specifically for accessibility.

  • Compliant cabin dimensions (e.g., 1100mm x 1400mm+)
  • Automatic sliding doors with compliant clear openings
  • Tactile and Braille control buttons at accessible heights
  • Audible voice announcements and visual floor indicators
  • Handrails and compliant interior mirror placement
  • Level landing access with minimal gap tolerances
