FAQs

Zenith DDA questions

What size is the Zenith DDA car?

1100 × 1400 mm.

How fast is the Zenith DDA?

0.3 to 1.0 m/s, using a highly reliable electric traction drive.

What power does the Zenith DDA need?

Single phase 230VAC. Only the E1 commercial lift needs three phase power.

Is it suited to busy buildings?

Yes. The Zenith DDA is built specifically for high-traffic commercial environments and handles high cycle counts while maintaining a sleek, professional office design.

How do I get a commercial quote?

Provide details about your project and our commercial estimators will prepare a tailored proposal, typically within 48 hours.

How long does installation take?

Installation of the Zenith DDA typically takes 2–4 weeks.
