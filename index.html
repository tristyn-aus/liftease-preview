FAQs

More in our home lift planning guide, or call 1300 81 01 47.

What warranty do your lifts come with?

A Factory backed 5 Year Parts Warranty on all our full elevators, and 2 years on the Levare compact lifts.

Are your lifts built to Australian Standards?

Yes. Our elevators are built to AS 1735, with full adherence to Australian Lift Standards. Our commercial lifts are DDA compliant, with tactile controls, auto doors and compliant cabins.

Can I get funding for a home lift?

NDIS home modification funding can cover a lift for eligible participants where it's assessed as reasonable and necessary. If you're approved for Support at Home, the AT-HM scheme can contribute up to $15,390. An occupational therapist (OT) has to prescribe the work, and we work with your OT on funding quotes.

How much does a home elevator cost?

Home elevator cost in Australia typically ranges from $36,000 to $100,000+, depending on the lift type, number of levels served, shaft requirements, cabin dimensions, and interior finish. These are indicative 2026 estimates for Melbourne and greater Victoria; final pricing depends on your property. We conduct a site assessment before quoting, and you get a free quote in 24 hours. Read our 2026 price guide.

Can you put a lift in an existing house?

Yes, a large percentage of home lifts are retrofitted.

Does a home lift need a pit?

Most modern home lifts need only a shallow pit, somewhere between 30mm and 200mm below the lowest floor. Some models run with no pit at all using a small ramp into the car.

Should I choose a hydraulic or electric home lift?

Both work well. Hydraulic lifts like the Vector Pro are proven and smooth; electric drives like the Atlus screw drive and the Zenith Ultra belt drive need no hydraulic oil. The right one depends on your space and floors. Compare hydraulic vs electric.

What happens if the power goes out?

Home lifts have a battery backup that brings the car to the nearest landing and opens the door, so nobody is left sitting in a stopped lift.

What maintenance is required?

We recommend a minimum of one service per year, twice-yearly for heavily used lifts, or as outlined by the manufacturer's servicing schedule.
